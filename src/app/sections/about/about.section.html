Formed in New York in 2026, When Light Breaks is an independent four-piece alternative metal band founded by vocalist Blegh Lady and guitarist DVRK PRISM. After relocating from Spain to New York, the two musicians began shaping a new project alongside bassist Dreadful Gooz and drummer Morgan Simons.

The Music

Blending elements of metalcore, djent, and deathcore with cinematic atmospheres and electronic textures, When Light Breaks explores the fragile boundary between perception and reality.

At its core, the band is driven by the idea that everything we believe to be true can eventually collapse. Through themes of identity, human emotion, personal experiences, and social contradictions, their music challenges established perceptions and explores the complexities of the human condition.

Dark, unpredictable, and unapologetically intense, When Light Breaks seeks to create something that goes beyond a conventional listening experience, embracing contrast, emotional depth, and the unexpected.

Their debut single, “Pre-Human State”, arrives November 6, 2026, accompanied by an official music video directed by Tom Flynn. The release marks the beginning of the band's musical journey, with further singles and a full-length album planned for 2027.
