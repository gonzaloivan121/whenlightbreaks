When Light Breaks is an alternative metal project formed by multi-instrumentalist and producer DVRK PRISM, and vocalist Blegh Lady. Based in the United States, the group combines traditional heavy metal instrumentation with modern electronic elements, low-tuned guitar work, and a wide vocal dynamic ranging from melodic singing to extreme vocal techniques.

History

Prior to the formation of the group, both members pursued independent creative paths in music and visual arts. Chaparro, an experienced guitarist and audio engineer, previously performed with the metalcore outfit Eyes of Paradise before establishing his solo production handle, DVRK PRISM. Blegh Lady built an audience as an extreme metal vocalist, tattoo artist, and creator.

A significant turning point occurred when both members relocated from Spain to the United States. Following the cross-continental move, the duo consolidated their creative efforts into a unified band project. The relocation marked an expansion in their sonic scope, shifting from standalone production efforts to a fully collaborative alternative metal outfit that integrated their combined backgrounds in songwriting, digital audio production, and visual design.

Musical style and influences

The band's sound is categorized primarily as alternative metal, incorporating prominent elements of modern metalcore and djent. Their compositions typically feature heavy, syncopated guitar rhythms, ambient synthesizer layers, and sudden shifts between dense, technical breakdowns and melodic choruses. The vocal delivery alternates between aggressive screams and gutturals by Blegh Lady and structured vocal lines that emphasize narrative contrast.

Operating largely through an independent production model, the band writes, records, and produces its material in-house, retaining control over both its musical output and related digital media.
